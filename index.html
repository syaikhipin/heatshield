Standards: Stull (2011) wet-bulb · ISO 7243 WBGT · OSHA/ACGIH continuous-work limits. HeatShield gives environmental risk guidance only — it does not diagnose or assess any individual. Emergency symptoms (confusion, collapse, hot dry skin) = call emergency services immediately.
